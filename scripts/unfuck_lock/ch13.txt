Chapter 13: Records and Meters — Nothing You Can Bag

What this unfucks: the token tic. The thing where every number becomes a bag you can hold, flip, and dump on a chart.

If you've spent any time in crypto, the word token already gave you a facial tic. Keep it. That mouth means a balance. Fungible. Transferable. Number go up. A "non-fungible token" is a modifier that admits the thing wasn't a token in the first place.

We are not minting six flavors of bag.

Two words. We mean them.

A record is a unique signed vertex on the ledger. One of one. CAT is a record. A domain claim is a record. A till spark is a record. A closed loop is a record. You don't have "some CAT." You have this CAT, minted then, for that skill, on that DID.

A meter is a number over records. XP is global standing. L is local coupling at this door, in [0, 1]. IT is governance weight. Meters are not wallets. They don't transfer. They move when the records under them move.

DT is retired. It was a sixth bag looking for a job. Domain is a filter on records — minted as a claim, contestable, tied to a DID and a time. Not a token you hold.

If you saw an earlier draft with GT, RT, DT-as-bag, or a "canonical six," that draft is dead. Burn it.

XP — standing, not a wallet

XP is the output of the mint:

XP = R × F × ΔS × (w · E) × log(1/Tₛ)

R is rarity, not reputation. Reputation does not enter this formula. I will say that until the comment section dies of boredom.

How you get it: reduce entropy. Submit a claim. Survive the Core Loop. The formula does the rest.

How you do not get it: dollars, crypto, favors, Harvard, your dad, a loyalty punch-card.

You do not spend XP. You do not transfer XP. There is no δ = 0.02 "friction" because there is no transfer. Friction on a door that doesn't exist is theology.

Decay: about a percent a month if you stop closing loops. Standing is a live condition, not a trophy.

XP is global. Every network you touch. It is not local gold.

CT — this door, not gold coins

CT is local contribution standing inside a particular DFAO. How much you actually showed up here.

It is not a spendable currency. It is not gold coins. It is not what buys the coffee. The video-game analogy was cute and wrong. Spending gold does not map to this system, because nothing is spending.

CT does not have a five-factor mint that smuggles reputation (ρ) back into purchasing power. That was the contamination we spent a year killing. Recognition (XP) and local standing (CT) and the till expression (EP) are separate on purpose. Put reputation in the money and you have FICO with extra steps.

EP — the product, then it's gone

At the till:

EP = XP × L

L is local coupling, a fraction in [0, 1]. Patronage density. Local ΔS. Carbon drag. DFAO nesting. It is not loop-closure speed. It is not "always ≥ 1 so we only ever amplify."

A traveler with no close at this door has L near zero. Zero times a mountain of XP is no discount. Come through every week, the local starts to run. Global times that door. Then the product burns.

It does not transfer. It does not ride a rail. Cash can still ring. The clerk does not decide. The computer tells them what the standing is worth on this ticket.

That is not a transaction in the Square sense. Nothing moved accounts. Standing expressed. Expression gone. There is no processor cut, no two-flat-plus-a-dime, no weekly rake. That fee flex was an oversell from a draft that thought we were a payments company. We are not.

IT — weight, not arcade tickets

IT is governance influence. Derived from sustained contribution. You cannot buy it, inherit it, or swap it.

It is not a coin you feed into the voting machine. Free governance is spam, so inactivity decays the weight. Using your voice is not "spending a token." Calling it a governance token was the old mouth. Stop.

CAT — a stamp, not a stack

CAT is a capability record. Portable across DFAOs. Not transferable between people. No decay on the fact that the work happened. Levels are receipts, not a shop.

You cannot buy CAT. You can import an existing license as an opening CAT — overlay, not a new priesthood — and it is contestable. Doctors don't start at zero because we were too pure to notice the world already had credentials. They start with a record that says who issued it, when, and that it can be burned if it's a lie.

Domain — a filter, not DT

Who knows this territory? Not a bag called DT. A domain claim on the ledger. Minted. Timed. Tied to a DID. If you stop showing up, the standing in that filter drops. You cannot hang it on the wall and retire from the work.

The stack

physical / system change
→ verified ΔS proxy
→ causal evidence on the DAG
→ XP standing (global)
→ local interpretation (CT / L)
→ economic expression (EP), then burn

Meanwhile, off to the side, not in the mint:

skill → CAT
governance → IT
domain → filter on records

That separation is the product. Value, money, reputation, skill, and power do not collapse into one number.

If someone hands you a slide that says six tokens, zero bullshit, they are holding a museum piece. Be polite. Then hand them this chapter.
