Appendix D: Records and Meters

If you see GT, RT, DT-as-bag, TT, Γ, or "canonical six tokens," that text predates this lock. Burn it.

RECORD — a unique signed vertex on the ledger. One of one.

METER — a number over records. Not a wallet.

XP — Experience (meter)
What: global standing from verified entropy reduction.
Formula: XP = R × F × ΔS × (w · E) × log(1/Tₛ). Physics floor still applies where a domain has one.
Transferable: no. There is no transfer, so there is no transfer friction.
Decay: yes. Roughly a percent a month if you stop.
Acquired by: verified ΔS. Full stop.
Spent on: nothing. XP is not spent.
Does not include: reputation.

L — Local coupling (meter)
What: how much this door and this person actually couple, right now.
Range: [0, 1]. Not ≥ 1. Not loop-closure speed. Not c_L.
Traveler with no local close: near 0.

CT — Contribution (local standing)
What: standing inside a particular DFAO. This door.
Not: a spendable token, gold coins, a currency, a rail.
Not: C × F × ρ × Δ × E with reputation in the mint.

EP — Emergent Product (expression)
Formula: EP = XP × L.
What happens: at the till, that product may discount the fiat ticket. Then it burns. It does not transfer. Cash can still ring. Clerk does not decide.
Not: Emergence Points you carry to the next town. Not Epistemic Proof (unfortunate collision; proof-layer is a record, letter later).

CAT — Capability (record)
Portable skill stamp. Not a bag. Not transferable between people. Work happened, so the stamp doesn't evaporate. Import existing licenses as opening CATs. Contestable.

IT — Influence (meter)
Governance weight. Not a coin. Not purchasable. Inactivity decays it. Using your voice is not an arcade spend.

DOMAIN — filter on records
Minted claim. Timed. DID-tied. Not DT. DT is retired.

TILL SPARK — record
The till event. EP expressed and burned. Overlay on the drawer that is already there. No Square cut.

CLOSED LOOP — record
The claim that survived.

Tₛ — slam window
Anti-farm. Not a firefighter speed bonus.

F — repetition of class
Count. Not a calendar.

c_L — slogan, not L
The t-shirt is XP ≥ ΔS / c_L². Pedagogical floor. Not the mint. Not the local meter L.
