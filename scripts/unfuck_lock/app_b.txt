Appendix B: Routing, Not Purchasing Power

There is a reliability ladder. It is per-domain. You can be used-to-this in code and new in thermodynamic at the same time. SignalFlow can route with it. Validators with a record of being right get harder jobs.

It does not multiply XP.
It does not mint CT.
It does not buy the coffee.

R in the XP formula is rarity. Not reputation. ρ is not a purchasing-power lever. If a draft put a 10-level "Newcomer → Foundational" ladder into the CT formula as ρ, that draft was washing reputation into money. We don't.

Standing at a door is CT and L, from work that closed, here. Reliability may affect who gets asked to look. It does not print discounts.

If you need a picture of the ladder for routing, use whatever labels you want. Do not put them on a price.
