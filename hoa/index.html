The truck is a monopoly on the scorebook

One PDF. A teacher fined for a flowerbed. Same photo three times. $200 for a weed on a hydrant. That's not standards. That's one office holding the only copy of what happened.

We keep our own book

Every closed job staples to the last one. Nobody can pull page three. That's the DAG. Their fine is a screenshot. Our book is the season. Anyone on a node can audit it.

Smart contracts instead of executives

You don't need a president with a mint key. If the common isn't mowed, post it. If someone mows it and neighbors sign it closed, it counts. If this, then that. A vending machine for a decision. No "we'll look at it next quarter." No clerk losing the paper behind the copier. The gavel is an if-then.

Community, not a fine mill

Hey — this yard's not mowed. Anybody wanna mow it? You mint XP. You contribute to the ledger. You reduce entropy. Garden MICRO. Lights MICRO. Mediation when two neighbors actually talk. Everything gets solved as a community building for the community.

Make them irrelevant first

When we're already covering the grass, the lights, the ditch, they can't collect meaning even if they still collect the check. Keep writing the old dues so they can't lien the house. Starve them of function. Show, over time, that we do a better job than they ever did. Then the votes can come in to dissolve. Maybe. Until then they're a costume.

The paper

Texas Property Code Chapter 209 applies whether they like it or not. §209.0041: 67% of the votes allocated to owners can amend the declaration. That statute supersedes a higher number in their papers. If they already wrote a lower %, that lower % wins. That's the dissolve door. Not a Facebook poll. §209.006: notice and a chance to cure before a fine. Recycled photos live here. §209.0093: 67% can strip foreclosure-for-dues; 10% can petition a special meeting for that vote.

Wyoming is the filing that actually exists for this shape: DAO LLC (W.S. 17-31) and DUNA (decentralized unincorporated nonprofit association, 2024). You live in Texas. You file the optional wrapper in Wyoming when you need a checking account or a name the county understands. The Engine does not wait for that stamp. The DFAO runs on the laptops either way. A lawyer does the filing. This is not legal advice.

Get their recorded CC&Rs, bylaws, articles, plat. Owners can demand dedicatory instruments. Hays County clerk has the recording. Drop the PDFs in the Sunset Oaks docs folder on GitHub when you have them — that's the rest of the pressure map. The City may have required an HOA on the plat. That would be a second lock.

Keep writing the old check until the paper changes. Unpaid assessments can still lien the house. Same as fiat: we run next to it until it is a costume. Starve them of jobs, not of a skipped bill.
