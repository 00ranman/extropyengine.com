Extropy Engine

Somebody did the work. Nobody wrote it down.

It happens at every job and every kitchen table. A neighbor gets helped, a shift gets covered, a fence gets fixed, and a week later it is like it never happened. No record, no standing, and the next time anyone asks what you are worth, you start from zero.

The Extropy Engine is a public record where finished work gets counted, in the open, where anyone can check it. That is the whole idea, and it takes about thirty seconds to see how it runs.

The receipt

The confirmation is the record

When both sides tap done, the confirmation lands on a public record. It is not a review, it is not a star rating, and it does not live on one company's server where it can be quietly edited. It is a receipt. It says this work happened, both sides signed it, and the loop closed.

Standing comes from how many of those a person has closed. Not from followers, not from a profile, not from who vouches for you in private. Anyone can walk the record and count for themselves.

The ask

Everything is free. The dollar funds the test.

The record, the school, the book, the music. None of it sits behind the dollar. It is free to anyone who shows up, supporter or not, and it stays that way.

What the dollar buys is the test itself: whether a public record of finished work can hold real weight, out in the open, with real people. Tests are not free to run. A dollar a month is the smallest way to fund one.

This page was built to talk you into that dollar, and the method is on the table, because there is only the one move: everything free, the test funded a dollar at a time, cancel anytime.
