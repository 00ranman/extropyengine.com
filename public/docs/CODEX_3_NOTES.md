## Two different clocks of death

5%/month is **IT**, not XP.

| Thing | What it does | Default (knob) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settle window | Provisional XP → standing XP | ~30 days was a starting number. Could be 5–40. Per-DFAO. |
| Late burn | Standing XP can still be destroyed | No expiry. Settled ≠ immortal. |
| XP decay | The pile shrinks even with no dispute | ρ = 0.01 per 30 loop cycles (~1%/month). Access economy. You do not spend XP. |
| IT decay | Governance weight bleeds if you vanish | ~5%/month. Different token. |

You keep working or the XP gets eaten. That mechanic was specified. It kept falling out of the story. Put it in 3.0.
