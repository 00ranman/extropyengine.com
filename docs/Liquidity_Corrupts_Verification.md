## Why liquidity corrupts verification

A verifier paid in a transferable unit has a second job: keep that unit liquid. The first job loses. Bitcoin made spending irrational once the unit was gold-against-the-dollar. Altcoins copied the exit. Carbon credits copied the certificate. Stablecoins became the cash rail.

Gesell: hoarding follows what does not leak. Beer: coordination without price as the only signal. Ostrom: rooms can govern a commons without a global market.

## How decay is the coupling
